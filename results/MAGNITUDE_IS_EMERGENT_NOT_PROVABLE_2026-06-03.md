## The one-sentence correction
Every magnitude-derivation probe we ran asked whether the dark-energy constant can be **DERIVED BY PROOF** (reduced to a closed-form / theorem from the
structure, the primitives, or the substrate). For a genuinely **emergent** quantity that question is **trivially "no" — that "no" is the *definition* of
emergence, not a finding about dark energy.** So those results are **category mistakes** (wrong instrument), **not load-bearing** results about whether
the program can produce the constant.

## Why (the instrument error)
Two different things hide inside the word "math":
- **math-as-mechanism** — writing the rules and stepping them. A computer run obeys this fully (deterministic, formal).
- **math-as-reduction** — a proof/formula that yields the answer *without stepping through*; a shortcut, and a shortcut always lives inside **one fixed
  theory (package)**.

Emergence escapes only the second. The corpus says exactly why:
- **Incompleteness** (`Tsiokos_2026_Six_Birds_for_Incompleteness...`): a fixed idempotent **package saturates** under iteration — repeated closure gives
  no persistent strict growth; *"genuine growth requires package change,"* and *"Gödel becomes the canonical fixed-package failure mode."* **P1/P2 are
  the mechanisms of package change.** A proof is locked in one package; it cannot reach content that exists only *after* the package has changed.
- **Strict extension** (`...Cantor_Shell`): a born layer `T_1` is **not definable from** its base `T_0` (`π_1` does not factor through `π_0`). New lawful
  content (the Cantor pressure-gap is the template) appears at `T_1` with no counterpart in `T_0`.
- **To Cast** (closure deficit): a *fully deterministic* map is one-way / random-looking **"relative to a bounded observer."** The output is determined
  and reproducible (lawful) yet unrecoverable inside a bounded budget/package. A proof is exactly a bounded observer with a fixed package.

**Running the six primitives ENACTS the package changes** (P1/P2 perform them as the system steps), so a run climbs the tower of strict extensions and
new lawful content — a constant, a scale, a regime — is **born** at each rung. A proof is one theory standing still and cannot traverse that tower. Hence
**a derivable constant is, by definition, not an emergent one**; the non-existence of the proof-shortcut *is* the genuineness of the emergence.
