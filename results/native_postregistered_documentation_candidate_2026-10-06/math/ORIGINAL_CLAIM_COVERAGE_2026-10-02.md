Using the unchanged original ODE and seed 1100 at lambda=0, tag probabilities

For independent IID patches with collapse probability p and an independent
true-tag probability q, let E0 and E1 be absence of a collapsed false and
true tag. Their probabilities are `(1-p(1-q))^N` and `(1-pq)^N`; their
intersection is no collapsed patches, with probability `(1-p)^N`.
Inclusion-exclusion gives

```
P_N = 1-(1-pq)^N-(1-p(1-q))^N+(1-p)^N.
P_(N+1)-P_N = pq[(1-pq)^N-(1-p)^N]
             +p(1-q)[(1-p(1-q))^N-(1-p)^N] >= 0.
```

The increment and monotonicity are mechanized for 0<=p,q<=1. The probability
derivation is the elementary model argument just given, not a formal probability
space in Lean. Exhaustive disjoint-outcome enumeration checks the expression
exactly for 96 finite rational cases. With N=2 and p=1, q=1/2 gives 1/2 while
q=1/4 gives 3/8. Thus even full-support tag distributions affect RS.
