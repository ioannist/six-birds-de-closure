# Physical interpretation and revised scope

On 2026-10-05 the user authorized downgrading or removing claims whose support
would require transfer from the mathematical model to physical dark energy or
cosmology. This decision concerns the later paper revision. The paper and
original runtime are unchanged.

The current paper's section11.1 already disclaims frame transfer. Nevertheless,
its abstract and title use broader language about the cosmological constant's
contingency, the absence of a value law and the physical coincidence problem.
The disclaimer does not supply the missing implications. The later revision
should retain dark energy as the motivating inference problem while restricting
theorems to their declared model or readout domains. A mathematical foundations
or methodology paper about dark-energy inference remains a reasonable scope;
a physical explanation of dark energy is not established by these results.

## Target boundary

The revised target is the exact model mathematics, original-input returns,
readout and information theorems, and their correct mechanizations. A derivation
of a physical cosmological constant or a gravitational/spacetime theory is
outside this target. Those questions need not be solved to complete the revised
review. Conditional optimization prices remain mathematical prices under their
declared assumptions; identifying a price with physical Lambda would require
additional premises and a physical bridge.

Removing that obligation does not discharge the remaining mathematical gates.
The regular coupling15 construction, combined original-sample theorem,
qualification, source integration, fresh registered builds, statement/axiom
audits and final provenance checks remain required. Private checked results are
distinguished from final registered integration.

## Interpretations supported within the models

- A finite diagnostic budget can conceal eventual convergence. The same
  original coupling15 orbit fails every four-test conjunction at updates1
  through60 and eventually converges. This does not mean every component test
  fails individually. See [the checked return](GROUND_NATIVE_STABLE15_FULL_RETURN_2026-10-05.md).
- Nonconvergence need not be unbounded runaway. The original coupling20/30
  trajectories approach attracting two-cycles, with unit states. These are
  results for the checked mathematical dynamics and original inputs.
- A coarse verdict can leave parameter determination ambiguous. Fifteen
  original background controls pass the sixty-update diagnostic, and all
  eighteen have limiting orbits. Neither Boolean readout recovers every control.
  Richer readouts or an additional selection criterion may distinguish them.
  See [the exact original-readout result](GROUND_ORIGINAL_AUDIT_NONIDENTIFIABILITY_2026-10-05.md).
- Determining both declared readouts requires preserving enough information
  to recover all four coordinates in the proved carrier. This is recoverability,
  not a lower bound of four physical storage fields or four encoding dimensions.
- The CPL identities give a smooth positive density/history representation
  across the declared crossing. They do not establish absence of ghosts or
  dynamical stability. The representation and the physical mechanism have
  separate proof obligations.
- The growth/records model earns a parameter window relative to its specified
  dynamics, population and record rule. Such a window characterizes that rule;
  it does not alone predict a physically realized parameter or its probability.

## Philosophical use

These results support a methodological distinction between a mechanism, its
recorded readouts, and a rule for selecting among compatible models or inputs.
Applied to cosmology, that distinction is a question to investigate: what does
a chosen observable determine, what information has been discarded, and what
additional laws or evidence would identify the mechanism?

Closure prices and record-admissibility windows can be discussed as analogies
for effective descriptions and selection constraints. No theorem here returns
those analogies to the actual universe. The results do not establish that dark
energy is emergent, uniquely selected, or merely a representation artifact.
Changing the scope also does not restore the contradicted background-blindness,
universal-runaway or measure-robustness claims.
