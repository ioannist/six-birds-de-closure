## The finite sweep is a budget verdict

The original cosmological-constant paper's abstract and section 6 use the
15-of-18 split, the failures at −5, −3 and +10, and a coupling control to claim
intrinsic contingency and absence of an in-layer value law. Three Stones v3
withdraws the interpretation of these finite-budget splits as existence
boundaries. It reports all eighteen background cases converging at 120
iterations and fixed points for all twelve sampled coupling values.

The present repo's repairs agree with that withdrawal and establish stronger,
precisely scoped mathematical replacements:

- All eighteen original rounded background cases converge in the exact native
  orbit model. Fifteen pass the particular sixty-update diagnostic. Its pass
  count is a budget-dependent diagnostic count, not an admissible-set boundary.
- Fixed-point existence and convergence of a specified iteration are different
  targets. Coupling 15 eventually converges from the original seed despite
  failing all budgets 1 through 60. Couplings 20 and 30 have bounded two-cycle
  limits under the declared update, rather than norm runaway. Their failure to
  converge does not negate the separately established fixed-point existence.
- Neither the sixty-update Boolean nor the Boolean encoding of actual limit
  existence recovers the original background control. This quantifies over
  decoders of those particular readouts only. Additional selectors, richer
  traces and other law classes remain possible.

## The generation-count comparison has no imported theorem

Three Stones v3 expressly reports no generation-blindness theorem. Its declared
N = 1,…,4 probe has mixed dependence: several predicates remain unchanged under
replication, while atomicity and route-incidence fail for N ≥ 2. Neither
failure to select N = 3 nor invariance of some predicates proves that the whole
grammar is blind to N.

Consequently the original abstract, section 6, conclusion and website statement
that Λ's value classifies with the Standard Model's generation count cannot
remain theorem-backed claims. No Lean theorem in this repo repairs that
upstream dependency. It is excluded from the revised mathematical target.
An informal comparison between measured inputs may be made only as an analogy,
without claiming shared proved blindness or intrinsic contingency.
