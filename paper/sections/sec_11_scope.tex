\hypertarget{limits-of-the-results}{%
\subsection{Limits of the results}\label{limits-of-the-results}}

The models are small and declared. The allocation model has 32 patches
and a sum budget; the record window uses linear growth, a fixed collapse
threshold and an imposed erasure rule; the lattice model has eight
sites. Their theorems hold for these models. The orbit classification
covers the sampled offsets and couplings, not every intermediate value.
Several statements are certified by exact computations outside Lean, and
the sampled sweeps, measure tables and audit fits are floating-point
results without rigorous error bounds. The staging audit is calibrated
with twenty mocks at a fitted anchor, so its false-alarm level is not
guaranteed, and its detection rates are empirical. Results imported from
other Six Birds papers frame the questions but are not proved here.

\hypertarget{open-mathematical-problems}{%
\subsection{Open mathematical
problems}\label{open-mathematical-problems}}

Three problems are natural next steps. First, classify the orbits of the
lattice model on whole intervals of offset and coupling, including the
transition between convergence and two-cycles between \(\kappa=15\) and
\(\kappa=20\). Second, mechanize the global existence of the constrained
ledger flow of Section 6.4, which is currently a paper-level argument.
Third, give the staging audit a calibration with a guaranteed level for
a composite null whose nuisance parameters are fitted to the data.
