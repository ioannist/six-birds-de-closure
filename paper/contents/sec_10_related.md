# 10. Relation to other work

**Unimodular gravity and vacuum-energy sequestering.** The allocation model of Section 4 reproduces, in a finite setting,
the pattern in which a global constraint with a Lagrange multiplier keeps a uniform energy offset from affecting the
state [@HenneauxTeitelboim1989; @KaloperPadilla2014]. The closure-price theorem adds a general condition for when such a
multiplier is positive, and the gauge theorem of Section 6.4 shows that in a closed-ledger model the absolute value of the
multiplier is not recoverable from the invariant record. Like unimodular gravity, neither result fixes the value.

**Anthropic and measure arguments.** The record window has the shape of Weinberg's bound [@Weinberg1987]: structure
formation limits $\lambda$ from above without placing the observed value near the limit. The measure probe makes the
dependence on the prior explicit [@MartelShapiroWeinberg1998; @BoussoPolchinski2000]. Section 7.3 proves that
smoothness near zero does not guarantee concentration, and that reweighting only the frozen base-grid values, with full
support and the stipulated conditional seed weights kept, cannot meet the fixed top-decade concentration criterion. The window and its first-record
ratio bounds are exact theorems about the model; the prior remains an input.

**Backreaction and averaging.** A companion pre-registration [@TsiokosDarkEnergy2026] treats dark energy as a correction
that appears when a homogeneous completion fails to commute with inhomogeneous evolution, and the audits of Section 8 are
built from its discriminators. The growth result is relevant to any program looking for dynamical signatures in structure
data: on the surveyed probes the decisive within-data-set comparison is not available, and an apparent strong-regime
residual is sensitive to nonlinear modeling.

**Holography.** Section 4.4 states the precise finite relation between min-cut area and an optimization price. Both are
outputs of a duality, but area is an optimal value under a declared scaling and the closure price is a multiplier; the
proposed shared composition rule for entanglement and area fails on an explicit example. We use the relation only as an
analogy.

**The Six Birds program.** Earlier work applied the same calculus to the structure of the Standard Model and to the
relation between quantum mechanics and general relativity [@TsiokosSixBirds2026]. That work expected questions about
contingent quantities to yield windows and statements about what is undetermined, rather than value laws, and questions
about budgets to yield structural relations. The results here fit that expectation: the budget question gave a theorem
about prices, and the value question gave readout-relative nonidentifiability and a gauge result. They do not prove it,
since the value question was answered for specific readouts and a specific ledger, not for all possible laws. This study
also reused the earlier framework deliberately, so it tests the framework's consistency and reach rather than providing an
independent confirmation.
