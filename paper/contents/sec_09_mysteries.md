Read across the rows, the table shows the demarcation the title refers to. The form of the constant slot, as a price of a
binding global budget, is a theorem about optimization problems. The window of values that allow records is a theorem
about a declared toy universe and its record gate. The value itself is not determined by the readouts of the lattice model, and becomes a
gauge coordinate once a global ledger is added. What would fix it, and with what probability, is left to inputs that the
models do not contain: a selection rule or a prior with explicit global content. The data audits are consistent with a
constant correction on current geometry, flag structured dependence between supernova and BAO subsets, and identify the
data a decisive growth test would need.
