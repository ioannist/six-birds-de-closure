## The one-sentence thesis

> Dark energy decomposes, under one frozen layer-agnostic structural grammar, into a **thin forced skeleton** (the constant-slot term is the
> shadow price of a binding global closure budget — computed in a controlled carrier, with the vacuum-shift screening pattern and slack collapse),
> a **record-stability admissibility window** (computed: strict, two-sided, Weinberg-shaped), and a **provably contingent value** (no in-layer
> value-law — the same moduli class as the SM's generation count), with the empirical tensions typed as **calibrated audit questions** (executed on
> real data: the constant-rewrite null that explains ΛCDM's success, plus a powered audit that detects the known SN-vs-BAO tension), and the
> coincidence residue **isolated to a single question** about the prior over the λ-modulus.

## Genre claim (the tie to the companion)

The companion established that the grammar **forbids different things in different places**: contingent selections support demarcations and
blindness theorems, not value-laws (F47/F26); co-sourcing refinements support positive relations (where its E=mc²-shaped RT recognition landed).
Dark energy's value sits on the *contingent-selection* side; its *form* sits on the *currency-constraint* side. The expected result-genres are
therefore: **PROVE-BLIND for the value, RECOGNITION for the form, COMPUTE for windows and audits** — and that is exactly what lands. This is the
third substrate for the one-grammar thesis (n = 3), with the differential-genre prediction honored again.

## Exclusion rule (binding for all sections)

The paper presents the program's results and the robustness steps taken *within* them (negative results included: the failed coincidence pinch,
the growth-chart boundary, the audit nulls, the staging-audit power lesson). It does **not** present, reference, or allude to exploratory work
products outside this path. Honest-negative ≠ wrong-premise: the former are results, the latter are absent.
