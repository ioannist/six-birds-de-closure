\hypertarget{appendix-a-model-specifications}{%
\section{Model
specifications}\label{appendix-a-model-specifications}}

All models are finite and deterministic given their seeds, run with
single-threaded numerical libraries, and reported in model units. A
\emph{validator} is an independently written script that re-derives a
verdict from the raw output tables; a nonzero mismatch count fails the
run.

\hypertarget{a.1-the-allocation-model-section-4}{%
\subsection{The allocation model (Section
4)}\label{a.1-the-allocation-model-section-4}}

\textbf{Inputs.} \(N=32\) patches indexed \(i=0,\ldots,31\), with fixed
heterogeneous coefficients
\[w_i=1.15+0.35\sin(0.37\,i+0.2)+0.08\cos(1.13\,i),\qquad c_i=0.18+0.22\,\bigl(0.55+0.45\cos(0.29\,i+0.4)\bigr).\]
No random numbers are used. These formulas generate the coefficients in
floating point; the saved binary values are reified as exact rational
inputs in Lean.

\textbf{Problem.} Maximize
\(\sum_i\bigl[w_i\log(1+x_i)-\tfrac12c_ix_i^2+b\,x_i\bigr]\) over
\(x_i\ge0\) with \(\sum_ix_i\le B\). The optimality conditions are
\(u_i'(x_i)=p\) for \(x_i>0\), \(u_i'(0)\le p\) for \(x_i=0\), \(p\ge0\)
and \(p(B-\sum_ix_i)=0\). The solver finds the price by a bracketed
scalar root of the allocation equation, decides activity from the
optimizer of the shifted, unconstrained problem, and treats the zero
budget separately.

\textbf{Sweep and readouts.} Ten budgets from \(8.90\) to \(66.73\); the
three largest exceed the unconstrained total and are slack. The price is
read from the optimality conditions and, independently, as minus a
central finite difference of the optimal cost
\(V(B)=-\max\sum_iu_i(x_i)\). The largest optimality residual in the
recorded sweep is \(1.50\times10^{-12}\) (\(1.11\times10^{-12}\) in a
later replay), and the largest price--derivative discrepancy is
\(3.56\times10^{-11}\).

\textbf{Offset leg.} The same offset \(b\,x_i\) with \(b=\pm0.2\) is
applied with the global budget at \(B\approx22.24\) and without any
budget. Floating results: with the budget, allocation shift at most
\(5.7\times10^{-14}\) and price shift \(b\) to within
\(3.3\times10^{-14}\); without it, allocation shifts of \(0.56\) and
\(0.67\).

\textbf{Controls.} Per-patch budgets of equal total give unequal prices
(\(0.494,\,0.548,\,0.568,\ldots\)); slack budgets give price zero; a
closed-form instance is unit-tested. The exact theory (unique optimizer,
price monotonicity, \(C^1\) value with \(V'=-p\), offset screening on
\([-\tfrac14,\tfrac14]\), the original inputs and both controls) is
mechanized (Appendix C).

\hypertarget{a.2-the-record-window-model-section-5}{%
\subsection{The record-window model (Section
5)}\label{a.2-the-record-window-model-section-5}}

\textbf{Background.} \(H^2=a^{-3}+\lambda\); integration from
\(a_0=10^{-3}\) to \(A=20\). For \(\lambda<0\) the expansion turns
around at \(a=|\lambda|^{-1/3}\); turnaround at or before \(A\) triggers
erasure.

\textbf{Patches.} \(N=64\) per seed; growth
\(\ddot D+2H\dot D=\tfrac32a^{-3}D\) with \(D(a_0)=1\),
\(dD/da(a_0)=1/a_0\); amplitudes \(m_i=s\,|z_i|\) with \(z_i\) standard
normal and \(s=10^{-3}\); independent tags with probability \(1/2\).
Unit test: in the matter era the growing mode \(D\propto a\) is
recovered.

\textbf{Records and predicate.} Patch \(i\) collapses when
\(m_iD(a)\ge\delta_c=1.686\), freezing its tag. A seed passes if
collapsed patches with both tags exist at \(a=A\) and no erasure
occurred; a value of \(\lambda\) passes if at least 10 of 12 seeds pass.
In the exact theory the decimal threshold is \(\delta_c=843/500\) and
all 768 realized amplitudes and tags are reified.

\textbf{Sweep.} A signed grid over eight decades in \(|\lambda|\),
twelve seeds per point, with edges refined by bisection to a factor
\(2.28\). Sampled results: lower edge between \(-1.18\times10^{-4}\)
(inside) and \(-2.7\times10^{-4}\) (outside), upper edge between
\(2.33\) and \(5.31\); inside witnesses for \(N=32\), \(64\) and \(128\)
at \(0.83\), \(2.33\) and \(2.67\). Across positive admitted grid
values, the median coincidence ratio over seeds ranges from about
\(3.5\times10^{-6}\) to \(2.1\), spanning about 5.8 decades.

\textbf{Exact results.} Window \((-1/8000,\lambda_+]\) with
\(3<\lambda_+<17/5\); eternal window \([0,u_\infty)\) with
\(3<u_\infty<3.4\); population brackets \((1.3,1.7)\), \((3,3.4)\),
\((5,6.25)\); starvation for \(\lambda\ge32\); first-record bounds of
Proposition \ref{prop:nopinch}; regularity in threshold and amplitude
scale on \([1.2,2]\times[\tfrac13,3]\).

\textbf{Controls.} Zero amplitudes give no collapses; constant tags fail
the both-tag seed predicate; the outside witnesses fail for the stated
reasons; the validator re-derives every sampled verdict.

\hypertarget{a.3-the-measure-probe-section-7}{%
\subsection{The measure probe (Section
7)}\label{a.3-the-measure-probe-section-7}}

\textbf{Inputs.} The A.2 model, with 36 logarithmically spaced positive
values inside the window, eight negative values and zero. Per seed and
value: pass flag, \(N_{\rm rec}\), \(f_{\rm coll}\), \(a_{\rm rec}\),
\(a_\lambda/a_{\rm rec}\) and \(R\).

\textbf{Classes and weights.} M1 to M5 as in Section 7.1, with cell
masses from trapezoid widths in \(\lambda\) or \(\log\lambda\). Each
cell mass is divided equally among the twelve seeds, each passing seed
contributes its own readout with its own record weight, and quantiles
are left inverses of the weighted empirical distribution function.
Logarithmic classes use the cutoff \(10^{-5}\).

\textbf{Criterion.} A class concentrates \(R\) if
\(P(\log_{10}R\ge\max-1)\ge0.8\) and the central 90\% width of
\(\log_{10}R\) is at most \(1.5\) decades. Results are in Section 7.2;
robustness variants \(N=32\), \(N=128\) and \(\delta_c=2\) give no
concentrating class.

\hypertarget{a.4-the-lattice-model-section-6}{%
\subsection{The lattice model (Section
6)}\label{a.4-the-lattice-model-section-6}}

\textbf{State and dynamics.} A complex field \(\psi\in\mathbb C^8\) on a
ring, with kinetic operator \((Ku)_i=u_i-\tfrac12(u_{i-1}+u_{i+1})\) and
gradient \((\nabla\psi)_i=\tfrac12(\psi_{i+1}-\psi_{i-1})\). The energy
density and potential are
\(T_{00,i}=|(\nabla\psi)_i|^2+V_{{\rm bg},i}|\psi_i|^2\) and
\(V_i=V_{{\rm bg},i}+\kappa T_{00,i}\). One update computes the
normalized lowest eigenvector \(\phi\) of
\(H=K+\operatorname{diag}(V[\psi])\), replaces it by
\(\phi e^{-i\arg z}\) with \(z=\langle\phi,\psi\rangle\) unless
\(|z|\le10^{-10}\), mixes with weight \(1/2\) and normalizes. The
initial field and the base background profile, whose values lie in
\([0.15,0.35]\), are those of the source.

\textbf{Test.} After 60 updates, four diagnostics must all be at most
\(10^{-8}\): the last update size; the fixed-point gap
\(\|\phi'-\psi\|\), where \(\phi'\) is the lowest eigenvector of the
final Hamiltonian after the source's realignment, which is applied twice
in this diagnostic; the eigen-equation residual
\(\|H\psi-\langle\psi,H\psi\rangle\psi\|\); and a sourcing residual
that, as implemented, compares the potential with itself and is
identically zero.

\textbf{Sweeps.} Offsets
\(c\in\{-8,-5,-3,-2,-1,-0.5,-0.2,-0.1,0,0.1,0.2,0.5,1,2,3,5,8,10\}\) at
\(\kappa=0.3\); couplings
\(\kappa\in\{0,0.1,0.3,0.5,1,2,3,5,10,15,20,30\}\) at \(c=0\).
Sixty-update outcomes: offsets \(-5\), \(-3\) and \(+10\) fail;
couplings \(15\), \(20\) and \(30\) fail. The exact long-run
classification is \Cref{thm:orbits}. In the exact theory the decimal
parameters \(0.1\) and \(0.3\) are the rational values of their binary
floating-point representations.

\textbf{Ledger-closed extension.} Adds
\(Q_\kappa=\sum_i(\rho_i+\tfrac\kappa2\rho_i^2)\) and its multiplier;
see Section 6.4. The allocation form maximizes a strictly concave
utility on the simplex \(\sum_ix_i=B\) in ledger coordinates
\(x_i=\rho_i+\tfrac\kappa2\rho_i^2\), and its explicit admitted pair has
\(\kappa=2\), densities \((1/2,1)\), ledger amounts \((3/4,2)\), budget
\(11/4\), and backgrounds \((0,1/2)\) and \((1,3/2)\) with prices
\(3/2\) and \(5/2\).
