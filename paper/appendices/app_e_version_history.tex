\section{Version
history}\label{appendix-e-version-history}}

\textbf{Version 1 (17 June 2026; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20713255).} First
release.

\textbf{Version 2 (7 October 2026; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23218104).}
Rewritten throughout for readability, with a new abstract and new or
redrawn figures, and updated to the mathematical audit of Appendix D.
The substantive differences from version 1 are:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \emph{Value of the constant.} Version 1 read the lattice model's
  sixty-update pass/fail table as showing that the background offset has
  no in-layer value law, that it is ``bounded moduli'' with no possible
  selector, and that a coupling control discriminates moduli from
  constrained parameters. The exact analysis shows that all eighteen
  offsets converge and that the coupling value 15 converges as well,
  while 20 and 30 approach two-cycles. Version 2 replaces those claims
  by the orbit classification, the readout nonidentifiability corollary
  and the gauge theorem of the ledger-closed extension (Section 6).
\item
  \emph{Imported comparisons.} The comparison of the value of
  \(\Lambda\) with the Standard Model's generation count, the
  record-stability duties concerning proton decay, monopoles and
  tabletop entanglement tests, the identification of min-cut area with a
  multiplier, the shared entropy--area composition rule and the
  noncommutation of the uniform completions are no longer used; the
  argument that an emergent value cannot be derived by proof is
  withdrawn. Section 4.4 and Section 6.5 state the precise finite
  results.
\item
  \emph{Measures.} The pushforward now weights individual record-bearing
  seeds; every row of the measure table changed, no class concentrates
  the coincidence ratio in any variant, and the claim that a prior
  non-singular near zero would make the coincidence typical is replaced
  by the counterexample of Section 7.3.
\item
  \emph{Window.} The sampled two-sided window is replaced by the exact
  window of \Cref{thm:window}. The infinite-horizon window is half-open,
  \([0,u_\infty)\); erasure is an imposed rule at turnaround; and the
  tag distribution affects the record probability.
\item
  \emph{Closure price.} The theorem is now mechanized in general form
  rather than through finite witnesses, and the adequacy residual is
  treated for singular native probes; the allocation coefficients are
  described as fixed arrays; and offset screening is stated with the
  condition that the shifted price stay nonnegative.
\item
  \emph{Audits.} The staging threshold uses a conservative rank rule
  (cutoff \(8.879\) instead of \(8.328\)), with unchanged verdict; the
  explanation of why coherent alternatives were not detected is
  narrowed; the supernova-only route residual is \(0.417\) (version 1:
  \(0.303\)); the growth shell census and HSC price range are corrected;
  the phantom-crossing statement is restricted to the existence of two
  regular descriptions; and the rewrite's BIC is reported.
\end{enumerate}
